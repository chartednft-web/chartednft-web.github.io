The Collection
Built
Different.

CHARTED is a 2,100-piece generative art collection built from real historical price data spanning 1999 to 2026. Every major market moment of the modern era — every bubble, collapse, and parabolic run — rendered as unrepeatable generative art.

We didn't generate images. We built 19 rendering engines from scratch. Each one is a completely separate visual world the algorithm steps into and draws inside of. Same price curve, 19 different outputs. That's not rarity — that's architecture.

Supply is 2,100 — not arbitrary. Bitcoin's hard cap is 21,000,000. Divide by 10,000. If you got that immediately, you understand what kind of collection this is.

01
Seeded Determinism
Every card runs a seeded RNG locked to its token ID. Every stroke, every grain, every texture offset — deterministic forever. Same seed. Same card. Always. You're not buying a render. You're buying an algorithm with one permanent output.
02
Real Data. Real Emotion.
The curve on your card is the real curve. The numbers are the real numbers. 78 events. 1999–2026. We chose price data because no dataset carries more human feeling per data point. Fear, greed, euphoria, collapse — all visible in the line.
03
19 Rendering Engines
Heavy Pencil. Thermal. Matrix Rain. Woodcut. Vaporwave. Glitch. Blueprint. Zine. Silk Screen. Each one built from scratch — not filters, not styles. Actual rendering systems. The rarest systems are locked behind the rarest tiers.
04
Zero Shortcuts
No AI generation. No pre-made assets. No Photoshop. Every visual element computed. The grain on a Heavy Pencil card is 60,000 individually plotted pixels. Pressure simulation. Bezier curves. Drawn by code.

Common Questions
FAQ.
When is the mint?↓
Mint date is TBA. Follow @chartedNFT on X for the announcement. Whitelist applicants will be contacted before the public date is posted.
What is the mint price?↓
Mint price is 0.001 ETH on Ethereum Mainnet. You'll need an Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet etc.) with enough ETH to cover the mint price plus gas. Whitelist holders get priority access in the early window before public mint opens.
What chain is CHARTED on?↓
CHARTED is on Ethereum Mainnet (ERC-721). You'll need an Ethereum-compatible wallet — MetaMask, Rabby, or Coinbase Wallet — with ETH for the 0.001 ETH mint price plus gas fees.
Does the art change after mint? Can it be lost?↓
No. Every card is generated by a seeded algorithm tied to the token ID. The seed is permanent — the same input always produces the same card, forever. There is no IPFS link to expire, no server to go down. The output is computed from the seed, not stored. In 100 years anyone can regenerate your exact card from the token ID alone.
Can I see my card before mint?↓
The specific card you receive depends on your token ID, which is assigned at mint. However — every rendering style and event visible on this site is exactly how the final cards look. The collection preview shows real outputs from the actual engine. No post-processing, no enhancement.
What does being on the whitelist get me?↓
Whitelist gives you priority access — an early mint window before the public. It does not guarantee allocation if supply runs out before your window. We'll confirm details per applicant before mint day.
What is an Artifact? Are they really 1-of-1?↓
Yes. The 21 Artifact cards are true 1-of-1s — one edition per event, never repeated. They cover the most singular moments in financial history: Bitcoin Pizza Day, the Genesis Block, the Bitcoin Whitepaper, Satoshi's last post, the DAO hack, Mt. Gox collapse, and more. Each renders in the Gold Deed format, exclusive to this tier.
How does the supply of 2,100 relate to Bitcoin?↓
Bitcoin's hard cap is 21,000,000. Divide by 10,000 and you get 2,100. The supply is not arbitrary — it's a direct reference to the asset that inspired the entire collection. If you caught that before reading this, you already know what kind of collection this is.
Was AI used to generate the art?↓
No. Every visual element is computed by custom rendering engines written from scratch. No AI generation, no Stable Diffusion, no Midjourney, no pre-made assets. The Heavy Pencil engine alone plots 60,000 individually calculated grain pixels per card. The algorithm is the artist.
Where can I trade after mint?↓
Secondary market details will be announced post-mint. We'll also launch a holder gallery and trait explorer at this domain after mint completes.
